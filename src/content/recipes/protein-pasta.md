image: "@assets/recipes/protein-pasta.jpg"
imageAlt: High-protein pasta bowl
author: JAJM2006
prepTime: 10
servings: 2
calories: 550
protein: "48g"
diet: High-Protein

ingredients:
  list:
    - Chickpea or Protein Pasta
    - Lean Ground Turkey (93/7)
    - Marinara Sauce
    - Low-Fat Mozzarella
    - Garlic Powder & Italian Seasoning
  qty:
    - 6 oz
    - 8 oz
    - 1 cup
    - 1/4 cup
    - 1 tsp each

instructions:
  - Boil the protein pasta according to package directions.
  - Brown the ground turkey in a skillet over medium-high heat. Season generously with garlic powder and Italian seasoning.
  - Drain any excess fat, then lower heat and stir in the marinara sauce.
  - Combine cooked pasta with the meat sauce in the pan.
  - Top with low-fat mozzarella, cover for 2 minutes until melted, and serve.

notes:
  - Great for meal prepping ahead of long streaming sessions.
  - Add fresh spinach to the sauce for extra micronutrients without changing the flavor.
