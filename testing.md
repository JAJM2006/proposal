Building a physique or climbing competitive ranks in gaming isn't just a physical test—it's primarily a mental filter. When you rely on motivation, you fail on the days you don't feel like showing up.

## The Control Test: Input vs. Output

Stoic principles focus on separating what you control from what you don't:

- **What you control**: Your effort, meal prep, sleep, and workout consistency.
- **What you don't control**: Plateaus, bad gym days, or unexpected schedule disruptions.

> "You have power over your mind - not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength."

## The 3 Pillars of Long-Term Consistency

1. **Focus on the Process, Not the Outcome**: Muscle growth and skill acquisition take months of quiet repetition.
2. **Embrace the Discomfort**: The final two reps of a set are where progress lives.
3. **Audit Your Environment**: Surround yourself with people who share your standard.

### Action Steps for the Week
* Track your primary compound lifts.
* Hit your daily protein goal before worrying about supplement stacks.
* Log off early at least 2 nights this week for baseline recovery.
